The living-room question · Xbox vs. 2nd PS5
Get the Xbox Series S
…not a second PS5, and not the $799 Series X.
~$250$499after your Best Buy card
  • Solves a real gap: Roblox isn't officially on Switch 2 — but it runs natively on Xbox. That's your Maddie co-op box.
  • Game Pass is tuned to your taste: roguelites & indies land there constantly. Premium $14.99/mo or Ultimate $22.99/mo for day-one first-party.
  • A genuinely new ecosystem — a second PS5 just mirrors the library you already play on your PS5 Pro.
Why the Series S, not the Series X: your PS5 Pro + gaming PC already own the high-end. The living room only needs Roblox, couch co-op, and the Game Pass roguelite buffet — the S nails all three at half the price.
The timing catch: the next-gen Xbox ("Project Helix") is rumored for ~Nov 2027 and is fully backward-compatible. So don't sink $799 into a Series X now — a cheap Series S bridges you perfectly until then.
The honest counter: if you'd rather own zero new boxes, Switch 2 + PS5 Pro + your gaming laptop already cover you. But "new ecosystem + Roblox with your kid + $250 out of pocket" is a great reason to say yes.
